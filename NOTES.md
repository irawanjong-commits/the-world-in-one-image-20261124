## Per-story notes (facts as used in the image)

- **Lead — Chen Jining, China.** Xinhua reported on Tuesday that Shanghai
  party chief Chen Jining, a member of the Politburo, is under
  investigation — the fourth member of the top leadership body to be
  probed since 2025 (Reuters).
- **Zaporizhzhia, Ukraine.** An overnight Russian attack struck an
  apartment building in Zaporizhzhia: 2 killed, 22 wounded including 2
  children (Reuters, citing regional officials; the regional governor's
  office gave a higher wounded figure of 40 — the image uses Reuters'
  confirmed 22).
- **Gaza.** Israeli airstrikes on Tuesday killed Naeem Abu Nahel, director
  of Hamas' internal security apparatus, and six others, including two
  police officers (medics and police, via Just Security's Early Edition;
  Reuters/Al Arabiya reported at least six Palestinians killed).
- **Gazipur, Bangladesh.** Garment workers blocked roads and clashed with
  police on Tuesday; police fired tear gas and sound grenades and dozens
  of factories suspended operations. Workers demand the minimum monthly
  wage rise to 30,000 taka ($241) from 12,500 taka (Reuters).
- **Sanofi–Insmed.** Sanofi said on November 24 it would acquire US
  drugmaker Insmed for $41 billion in cash, $175 per share, a 46% premium
  (Reuters Breakingviews).
- **Bird flu, Washington state.** The WHO said on Tuesday it had been
  notified by the United States of three confirmed human bird-flu cases
  in Washington state, including one death (Reuters).
- **Tiangong / Shenzhou-24.** Pakistan's Zeeshan Ali is set to become the
  first foreign astronaut aboard China's Tiangong space station, flying
  on the Shenzhou-24 mission (Straits Times, via Horizon; One Turn).
- **Markets.** Nvidia's board reinstated founder Jensen Huang as CEO after
  pressure from President Trump; the stock's surge drove a broad rally —
  S&P 500 +4.7%, Nasdaq +6.5%, Nikkei +3.9% (Reuters Trading Day; Horizon
  market closes for 24 November 2026).
- **Ticker items.** Germany becomes the first country to gain access to
  Ukraine's Delta battlefield management system (letter of intent signed
  in Berlin, Nov. 24). UNSMIL called for an independent investigation
  into the attacks at Benghazi port. NATO chief Mark Rutte said the
  alliance will defend against threats in space after reports of a
  Russian nuclear-armed satellite. A 5.6-magnitude quake struck Nepal's
  Koshi province near the Chinese border. Heavy fighting in Ituri, DR
  Congo, trapped Ebola responders near a hospital for over 24 hours.
